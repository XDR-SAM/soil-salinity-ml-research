# Publication readiness

**Decision: requires further research before a confirmatory submission.**

The manuscript and reproducibility package are complete. The V3 proposal did not meet the frozen within-benchmark paired-superiority rule. The analysis reuses campaigns whose V1 outcomes were known. A third field campaign or geographically independent dataset should be held outside all method development. In addition, absolute high-EC errors and bidirectional transfer performance constrain deployment. The exact 2026 Satkhira comparison requires author-specific rows, inputs, folds and the scored prediction procedure.

The coverage result is directly established on these records. Method comparison and subgroup results are valid as reanalysis estimates under the archived split design, with uncertainty conditional on the assessed regions. There is no field-wide ranking or external validation claim.
