# Validation audit

Recomputed the complete prior archive from row predictions with an independent implementation using compensated summation. Verified the exact V1 training, validation and test memberships, disjoint labels, geographic buffer and nearest-training distances. The least spatial test-to-train distance was 1.161 km. Preserved the byte-identical prior output and reused its row and fold order. No held-out outcome was used to fit preprocessing, candidate models, or meta-weights. The identities and results of V1 were known at study design time.

A staged workflow verifies native-pixel QA and local observation dates, source completeness, forecast counts, fold-local transformations, per-family inner ranking, blending constraints, pooled and subgroup metrics, and numerical manuscript inputs. `site_audit.csv` records V1 residuals and optically missing sites by year and spatial group. `coverage.csv` quantifies the relation with observed EC. `subgroups.csv` includes EC ranges, campaign, block, distance and sensor availability. Scene opportunities distinguish absent Landsat footprints within the declared time window from an unresolved pixel with candidate coverage. The latter may reflect QA exclusion or source access and is not assigned a causal class without per-site observations.

The source cohorts were acquired in different months and contain no exactly repeated coordinates. This confounds geographic and seasonal change. Pixel support can differ from the composite soil footprint and source classifications are imperfect proxies for land cover.

The final seven-page PDF was rendered at 120 dpi and inspected page by page. The workflow's branch arrows, dense table, map legends and transfer legend were checked in the rendered document. The final log contained no overfull boxes, missing figures or unresolved references. Remaining TeX diagnostics relate to underfull float spacing and font initialization before embedded Times-style fonts were loaded.
