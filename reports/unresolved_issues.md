# Unresolved issues

- **Independent confirmation.** V3 is a nested reanalysis of prior outcomes. An untouched campaign or external field data are unavailable.
- **Exact Satkhira replication.** The pinned independent district overlay matches the two reported counts. This does not establish retained row, Landsat input, fold or calibration identity. The new XGBoost-plus-spline comparison is method-inspired.
- **Sensor recovery scope.** Landsat and static terrain are used in the main benchmark. Sentinel-1 RTC and historical reanalysis endpoints were probed. A complete radar extraction with comparable orbit/terrain QA and an operationally time-valid weather dataset were outside the executed benchmark. An independent land-cover or hydrography inventory was unavailable, so optical masking is not treated as a causal land-cover attribution.
- **Operational latency.** The optical cutoff uses observation times. Product processing, publication and delivery delays were not tested.
- **Sparse extremes.** Individual-site predictive intervals, broad seasonal transport, and reliable error bounds in each high-EC subgroup require additional data.
- **Submission venue.** The IEEEtran conference class is used directly. The prior pasted template and illustrative source image were unavailable. No venue-specific acceptance or approval is asserted.
