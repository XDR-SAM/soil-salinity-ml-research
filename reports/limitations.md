# Limitations and reviewer checks

1. **Exact published comparison.** Matching spatial subset counts are insufficient to verify exact sample identity, Landsat extraction, folds or GAM scoring. The public report distinguishes the local coordinate/satellite comparisons from the 2026 preprint. See [comparison.md](comparison.md).
2. **Target validity.** Both workbook records specify the 1:5 extract and instrument. Concentrations, EC from a saturated paste and ROC AUC are different endpoints. The numerical EC values and units are preserved.
3. **Transportability.** There are only two campaigns and no coordinate-identical cross-year repeat. Month, year, target distribution, and site support differ. The two transfer directions quantify performance on these observations rather than variance over future campaigns. Both transfer directions were specified before metrics were generated.
4. **Spatial validation.** One geometry and six groups provide low effective sample size. The fixed 1 km buffer prevents near-site leakage under spatial CV and in the transfer sensitivity. It cannot prove spatial independence. Held-out losses are conditional on the sampled sites and training folds. Block bootstrap intervals do not include retraining variance or establish per-site prediction coverage.
5. **Predictor validity.** Native pixels do not replicate the field sample footprint, and the surface elevation product may include vegetation or structures. Optical missingness is handled with training-fold median imputation. Moisture, crop stage and cloud residuals can influence reflectance. The workflow uses archived observations by acquisition date and does not evaluate data-delivery latency.
6. **Interpretability.** Joint permutation estimates model reliance under a distribution perturbation. Correlated group substitutions can create unrealistic predictor combinations. The findings do not identify causal effects.
7. **Implementation decisions.** IDW was executed as the interpolation comparator. EBK, additional dynamic environmental predictors, stacking, GAM calibration, deep networks and per-point prediction intervals were outside the executed design. No synthetic training labels were created.
8. **Submission review.** The supplied author and supervisor details appear in the manuscript. No email, ORCID, funding or ethics declarations were inferred. The team should review those details and the target venue's requirements before submitting.
